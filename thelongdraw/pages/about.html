This is The Long Draw.

We started The Long Draw because everything got too fast, too cheap, and too disposable. Because the things people carry every day stopped meaning anything.

The Long Draw is named for the slow, deliberate pull — of a cigarette, of a flask, of a life lived without rushing. We curate goods for people who still believe the things you carry say something about who you are.

Every product on this site has been chosen by hand. If it doesn't belong in the same jacket pocket, it doesn't belong here. We look for things built before planned obsolescence was invented. Things that carry better after a year than they did on day one.
